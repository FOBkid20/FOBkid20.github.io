    Carbon Offsetting Travel

    Minimizing environmental impact is important to me. Beyond consolidating the majority of my investments into environmentally conscious or favorable companies, a primary way I try to minimize my personal carbon footprint is to purchase carbon offsets for travel. Every year, I track the travel I do via car, bus, train, and plane, calculate my carbon footprint, and purchase a corresponding number of carbon credits from a reputable source. (As of 2026, I purchase verified carbon credits through Gold Standard’s Climate+ Portfolio). I have fully calculated and offset 100% of out-of-state travel footprints for 2022 through 2025.

    Skills and Tools: Quantitative Carbon ModelingData LoggingESGVendor Due Diligence
