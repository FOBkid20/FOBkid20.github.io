Lexi Jen Ryan

LJR

Hello and welcome to my website! My name is Lexi Jen Ryan, and I'm a Technologist & Product Strategist living in New York City. With a background bridging Computer Science and Linguistics, my professional interests live at the intersection of natural language processing, machine learning, and human-centric design. I love building tools where structured logic meets human expression—whether that's designing a long-term product roadmap or diving into computational data. Most recently, I worked in Technical Product Management at the Bank of New York.

I hail from Southborough, Massachusetts. Between high school and college, I took a gap year in Manhattan studying musical theatre performance. Though I decided not to continue with theatre, I did fall in love with the Big Apple, where I now live with my cat Havana. Today, I'm privileged to be able to experience the magic of Broadway as an audience member, seeing roughly two shows per month. You can also catch me on the dance floor practicing social dances such as Conta, Blues, Swing, and Fusion dance.
